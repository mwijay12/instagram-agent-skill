## 8. Caption, CTA na DM (kwa `/ig-caption` na `/ig-dm`)

- **Job A** (Reel yenye hook kali): caption inafanya ask + context + search terms. Si hook ya pili.
- **Job B** (carousel / photo): line 1 ndiyo hook, ikatwe kwenye cliff, si katikati ya sentensi.
- Mstari wa kwanza unaonekana **~125 chars** kabla ya "... more". `caption.py` inakuonyesha dirisha hilo.
- Hashtags **≤5** (cap ya Desemba 2025), maalum, si `#viral #fyp`. Search terms kama maneno ya kawaida ndani ya sentensi: *"mfumo wa mauzo dukani"*, *"POS Tanzania"*, *"offline-first POS"*.

## 9. Repurposing: rekodi moja → wiki moja

Doc 05 inataka assets 12 kwa wiki kutoka recording moja. `/ig-plan` inapendekeza post 4–5 kwa wiki (angalau 3 Reels). **Mgongano huu ni wa kweli:** 12 post/wiki kwenye akaunti yenye followers 5 na 48, bila evidence yoyote, ni kelele.

Pendekezo la kit hiki:

```
Jumatatu: rekodi dakika 8–12 (sauti + screen) kuhusu tukio MOJA halisi la wiki
   ├─ Reel 1 (30s)      → hook #18 Cold Open Demo, code/screen halisi
   ├─ Reel 2 (20s)      → hook #12 The Objection, kutoka comment moja
   ├─ Carousel (8)      → muundo huo, kwa ajili ya saves
   ├─ Stories (3–5)     → poll / question box → DM funnel
   ├─ First comment     → link ya pili, si caption
   └─ log.md            → tarehe, hook id, mstari wa kwanza
Jumamosi: soma insights, andika outlier multiple na sends/reach
```

Hii ni post 3–4 za feed + stories za kila siku, na bado inatoa assets za LinkedIn/X/blog ukitaka. Ongeza kadri **evidence** inavyoonyesha, si kabla.

## 10. Vipimo (kutoka `/ig-audit`, si raw views)

| Kipimo | Jinsi | Inasema nini |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outlier multiple | views ÷ median views za akaunti yako | Je, ilikuwa hit halisi? |
| Hold@3s | waliobaki sekunde 3 ÷ walioanza | Daraja la hook |
| **Sends / reach** | shares ÷ reach | Ishara yenye nguvu zaidi unayoweza kupata |
| Follows / reach | follows ÷ reach | Je, profile ilibadili attention? |
| Non-follower reach | % | Je, ilisafiri nje ya followers? |

Akaunti zenye followers 48 na 5: **usilinganishe na 400K views za akaunti kubwa**. Kusanya akaunti ndani ya ~10x ya ukubwa wako, kisha pima multiple. Reel ya views 900 na sends 40 kwenye akaunti ya 48 followers ni ushindi mkubwa kuliko views 20,000 na sends 3.
