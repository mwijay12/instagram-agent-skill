06 · SEO ya kila post

ACT 06 / SEO

  • Keyword MOJA kwenye mstari wa kwanza wa caption, ndani ya herufi 125 zinazoonekana kabla ya '... more'.
  • Keywords na hashtag kwenye CAPTION, si kwenye comments - ndivyo Instagram mwenyewe anavyosema.
  • Hashtag 3-5 maalum, zinazozungushwa. Cap rasmi = 5 tangu 18 Desemba 2025.
  • Alt text kila picha. Ni accessibility; Instagram HAIJATHIBITISHA kuwa ni ranking signal - tunaiandika kwa sababu ni sahihi, si kama hack.
  • Reel 9:16, chini ya dakika 3, first 3 seconds inashika; keyword inasemeka na inaonekana kwenye captions zilizoburniwa.
  • Location tag Dar es Salaam / Kariakoo inapohusika.
  • Ask MOJA tu. Hakuna URL wala emoji bullets kwenye caption.
  • Original content tu: 10+ reposts za wengine ndani ya siku 30 = hutoki kwenye recommendations.

VIPIMO VYA KWELI: Watch time, DM shares (sends) na saves ndizo zinazobeba reach; likes ni za pili. Pendekezo: 3-5 post kwa wiki, niche moja. Haya ni heuristics za industry, si ahadi ya viral - retention graph yako baada ya post 30 ndiyo hakimu.

10 · Usalama kwanza, mara ya pili

ACT 10 / USALAMA

Akaunti ya zamani @mwijay.davie (~12K, wengi bots, ilibanwa baada ya automation na token ya Meta API iliyovuja) ni taarifa ya mmiliki, SI iliyothibitishwa hadharani. Iko kwenye 01-presence-audit.md §8 kama risk context tu - haijaingia kwenye caption, bio, prompt wala PNG yoyote.

  • Password mpya unique + 2FA kwa authenticator app + recovery codes offline.
  • Email ya account iwe na 2FA yake yenyewe kwanza.
  • Accounts Center -> Where You're Logged In -> logout wasiotambulika.
  • Accounts Center -> Apps and Websites -> Remove kila app usiyoitumia leo.
  • Meta App Dashboard -> reset App Secret, Require App Secret ON, revoke tokens; GitHub secret scanning kwenye repo zote. Rotate KWANZA, kisha safisha history.
  • Hakuna app ya 'free followers', hakuna auto-follow/auto-like/mass DM, hakuna password au 2FA code kwa mtu yeyote.
  • Recovery ikihitajika: instagram.com/hacked na Meta Account Recovery Hub tu. Hakuna support email wala hotline; DM za 'Meta Support' ni scam.
